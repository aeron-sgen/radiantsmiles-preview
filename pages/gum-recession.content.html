Recession is easier to feel with cold water than to see in a mirror. One tooth flinches, and then it does it again.

What has happened is that the gum has moved back and left part of the root uncovered. Root surface is softer than enamel.

It is more sensitive, it decays faster, and it collects plaque more easily. The gum does not creep back over it on its own.

Recession also often moves slowly, which is exactly what makes it easy to ignore. Change that gradual can be hard to see in a bathroom mirror.

Does treatment hurt?

The area is numbed before any work begins. Exposed roots can be tender afterwards. We will not promise comfort we cannot control.

Will my gums grow back?

Gum tissue does not return over the root by itself. The aim is to stop the recession going further and to protect what is exposed.
