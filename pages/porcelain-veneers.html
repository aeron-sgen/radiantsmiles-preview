Porcelain Veneers in Las Vegas

Thin custom shells bonded to the front teeth, for chips, gaps and staining that whitening will not lift.

Book an appointment Call the office

If you are thinking about veneers, you can probably name the tooth. The chip from years ago, the gap that never closed, the one tooth that stays darker.

Whitening does not fix any of those.

Bleaching lifts surface colour, so it does nothing for shape, for spacing, or for discolouration that sits inside the tooth.

So the thing gets lived with. People learn which side to turn in photographs, and it stops feeling like something you are allowed to raise.

The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. Dental visits track coverage directly, so people without benefits are simply seen less often.

That figure is about dental care in general, not about cover for cosmetic work.

Close the gap you keep noticing.

Veneers change shape and spacing across the front teeth, which is the part of your mouth you actually see.

Cover staining that bleaching will not lift.

Some discolouration is inside the tooth rather than on it. A veneer covers it rather than trying to remove it.

Handle it as one plan, in one practice.

Shape, spacing and shade are decided together, inside one practice, before any tooth is prepared.

01

We start with what you want changed.

In your own words, tooth by tooth, before anything clinical begins. Sometimes the answer is a simpler treatment than veneers.

02

We examine what is underneath.

Veneers sit on sound teeth. Decay, a failing filling or gum disease is treated first, and digital X-rays are taken where they help.

03

We prepare the teeth and take an impression.

A small amount of enamel is usually removed so the veneer sits flush. Enamel does not grow back, so this step cannot be undone.

04

The veneers are fitted, checked and adjusted.

Shade, shape and bite are checked in your mouth before anything is bonded for good.

What do veneers cost?

We quote after an examination. The figure depends on how many teeth are involved and on the condition of the teeth underneath. We do not publish treatment prices.

Does insurance cover them?

It depends on your plan and on why the veneer is being placed. Our insurance and financing page publishes the full list of named plans. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

Does it hurt?

Our own veneers page says the process needs little or no anaesthetic, captured 30 July 2026. Teeth can be sensitive between the preparation visit and the fitting, and we will tell you what to expect.
