Most people who grind their teeth do it while asleep.

You do not feel it happening.

What you notice is a sore jaw in the morning. Sometimes a dull headache at the temple, or a tooth that has started to feel flat.

Grinding can wear enamel down quietly. Enamel does not grow back. By the time a tooth chips or a filling cracks, the repair is a larger one.

That is the whole reason to deal with it early. Not because grinding is an emergency, but because it is slow, silent and cumulative.

Wear a guard shaped to your own bite.

It is made from an impression of your teeth, and designed to reduce the abrasive action of grinding while you sleep.

Keep the tooth you already have.

Protecting enamel now is simpler than rebuilding a worn or cracked tooth later.

Stop guessing what is causing the headaches.

We look at the wear pattern on your teeth and tell you what it shows, in plain terms.

We look first.

A clinician examines the wear surfaces, your bite and the joint. Digital X-rays are taken where they add something. You are told what is seen, not handed a plan.

We take an impression.

The guard is made to your bite, not pulled from a drawer of sizes.

We check the wear again later.

Grinding often tracks stress and sleep. Reviewing it over time tells us whether the guard is doing its job.
