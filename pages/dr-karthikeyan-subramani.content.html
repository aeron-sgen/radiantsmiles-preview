Approach

Orthodontic treatment is long, so the first conversation carries more weight than usual.

You are shown what your bite is doing now, and what the stages would be.

Where a case needs treating, he sets out what the treatment involves. Where waiting and watching is reasonable, he says that out loud instead.

How long treatment runs, and where it finishes, depends on the individual bite. Yours has not been assessed yet.
