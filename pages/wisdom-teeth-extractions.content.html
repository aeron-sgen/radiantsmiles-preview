Wisdom teeth arrive last, generally between the ages of 17 and 25.

By then the jaw is already full.

When there is not enough space, the tooth can become stuck in an undesirable position. Our own wisdom teeth page says the molar in front can be adversely affected, with decay, gum disease and possible bone loss.

Sometimes you feel that as a dull ache behind the last tooth. Sometimes you feel nothing at all.

A tooth that is only part way through the gum is hard to clean. Food and bacteria can become trapped under the gum.

That is where the trouble can start. A sore gum, a bad taste, or a swelling.

Cost is a large part of why people wait it out.

Dental visits track coverage directly. In 2023, 53% of adults with private dental insurance had a dental visit, against 16% of those with none. Among adults aged 65 and older, 55% have no dental benefits.

These figures are from the ADA Health Policy Institute, fetched 30 July 2026. They describe dental coverage across adults, not wisdom teeth specifically.

Chew on both sides again.

When the back of the jaw is sore, you start favouring one side without noticing. Dealing with the tooth takes that away.

Stop the same gum flaring up.

A partly erupted tooth can trap debris under the gum. Removing the tooth removes that trap.

Protect the molar in front.

An impacted wisdom tooth can adversely affect the second molar directly in front of it. That is a tooth you use every day, and keeping it healthy is the point of acting early.

What does a wisdom tooth extraction cost?

We quote after we have seen the X-ray, because the angle and the roots change the work involved. We do not publish treatment prices. A number given before the exam would only change afterwards.

Does insurance cover it?

Coverage depends on your plan, the tooth and how the extraction is coded. Our insurance and financing page publishes the full list of named plans. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

Does it hurt?

Our own wisdom teeth page publishes the anaesthesia options as local anaesthetic, intravenous sedation or general anaesthesia. Sedation methods are offered for this procedure at every one of our offices. Which of those options applies is decided at the examination. We will not promise you a pain-free procedure. We will tell you honestly what to expect on the day and afterwards.
