If you are thinking about veneers, you can probably name the tooth. The chip from years ago, the gap that never closed, the one tooth that stays darker.

Whitening does not fix any of those.

Bleaching lifts surface colour, so it does nothing for shape, for spacing, or for discolouration that sits inside the tooth.

So the thing gets lived with. People learn which side to turn in photographs, and it stops feeling like something you are allowed to raise.

01

We start with what you want changed.

In your own words, tooth by tooth, before anything clinical begins. Sometimes the answer is a simpler treatment than veneers.

02

We examine what is underneath.

Veneers sit on sound teeth. Decay, a failing filling or gum disease is treated first, and digital X-rays are taken where they help.

03

We prepare the teeth and take an impression.

A small amount of enamel is usually removed so the veneer sits flush. Enamel does not grow back, so this step cannot be undone.

04

The veneers are fitted, checked and adjusted.

Shade, shape and bite are checked in your mouth before anything is bonded for good.
