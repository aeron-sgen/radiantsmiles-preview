An examination reads surfaces.

It cannot see between two teeth that touch each other, under an old filling, or below the gumline.

That is exactly where decay tends to start. It is also where a hairline crack sits, quietly, until the day the tooth gives way.

So a mouth can look fine and still be losing a tooth. An image is how that gets settled rather than guessed at.

Coverage is the other half of the delay. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. Dental visits track coverage directly.

See the reason, not only the recommendation.

The image goes up on the screen and we point at the thing we are describing.

Treat the smaller version of the problem.

Decay found on an image can still be small enough for a filling rather than a crown or a root canal.

Get the image without a referral out.

The X-ray is taken in our own operatory and read by the clinician treating you. Nothing waits on an outside imaging appointment.

  1. 01

    We take an image when there is a reason to.

    A new patient exam, a symptom, a tooth we cannot read by eye, or a change we want to compare.

  2. 02

    The sensor goes in and the image appears in seconds.

    There is no film and no waiting for it to develop, so the appointment keeps moving.

  3. 03

    We look at it with you, not at you.

    The screen is turned so you can see the tooth being discussed and ask about it.

  4. 04

    We keep them and compare them.

    Two images of the same tooth a year apart show whether something is changing or holding steady.

What do X-rays cost?

For a new patient, the X-ray is part of the entry offer below. Beyond that we quote after an examination, because it depends on how many images are needed. We do not publish treatment prices.

Does insurance cover them?

Coverage depends on your plan. Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife and the Culinary Health Fund are among the 41 carriers listed on our insurance and financing page. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

Does it hurt, and how often do I need one?

We will not describe it as painless. There is no needle and nothing goes under the gum, though the sensor can feel bulky. How often depends on your history, so we take one when it will change what we do.
