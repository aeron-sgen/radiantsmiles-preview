Losing several teeth changes three things at once.

  • Eating narrows to what you can manage.
  • Certain sounds stop coming out cleanly.
  • And the lower face loses some of the support that used to hold its shape.

People adapt around all of it quietly. Meals get chosen by texture. Photographs get avoided.

It rarely arrives as a crisis, which is exactly why it goes unaddressed.

What do dentures cost?

We quote after the examination, because a partial for two gaps and a full upper are different pieces of work. We do not publish treatment prices. A number given first would only change later.

Does insurance cover them?

Coverage depends on your plan. Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife and the Culinary Health Fund are among the 41 carriers listed on our insurance and financing page. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

Does getting them fitted hurt?

The impressions do not. If teeth have to come out first, that is done with anaesthetic and the site is given time to heal. Early sore spots are adjusted out.

Will they slip?

Fit is what controls that, and fit is what the adjustment visits are for. Tell us where it moves and we work on it. We will not promise you a result we cannot see yet.
