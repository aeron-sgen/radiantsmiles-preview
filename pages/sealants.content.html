It is a shape problem before it is a habit problem.

Look closely at a back tooth. The chewing surface is not flat. It is a set of narrow grooves, and food can become trapped in them.

A brush does not reach the bottom of a narrow groove. So the one spot that collects the most is also the spot a brush cleans least well.

It is why a first cavity can turn up in a molar even in a child who brushes.

Access decides the rest. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. Dental visits track coverage directly.

Cover the groove before it becomes a filling.

A sealed surface has no deep channel left for food to pack into.

Get through the appointment without drama.

Nothing is drilled and nothing is numbed, which matters if your child is already nervous.

Have it checked at every visit after that.

A sealant can chip or wear, so we look at it whenever you come in.

  1. 01

    We check the tooth first.

    A sealant goes over a sound surface. If there is already decay in the groove, sealing it is the wrong move.

  2. 02

    The tooth is cleaned and dried.

    The coating only bonds to a clean, dry surface, so this step is done carefully.

  3. 03

    The coating is flowed into the grooves.

    It runs into the narrow channels and fills them, so the chewing surface becomes smooth and easier to brush.

  4. 04

    It is set before you leave.

    Our own sealants page says the material either hardens immediately or is cured with a special light.
