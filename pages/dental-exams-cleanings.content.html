Exams and cleanings run at all seven of our offices, so the appointment fits the day you actually have.

  1. 01

    We ask first.

    What hurts, what has changed, what you are worried about. Your medical history and your medications matter here, so we go through them.

  2. 02

    We examine.

    Teeth, existing fillings, gums, bite and the soft tissue of the mouth. What we find is written down and compared at the next visit.

  3. 03

    We take digital X-rays when they will change something.

    They may reveal decay between teeth and below the gumline, where the eye cannot reach.

  4. 04

    We clean.

    Soft plaque and hardened deposits come off, and then the teeth are polished. If the deposits go deeper than a routine cleaning reaches, we say so, and that is a different treatment.

  5. 05

    We tell you what we found.

    In order, in plain words, with the urgent separated from what can safely wait.

What does a cleaning cost?

We quote after an examination, because what a cleaning involves depends on what we find. We do not publish treatment prices. A number given before we look would only change afterwards.

Does insurance cover it?

Coverage depends on your plan. Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife and the Culinary Health Fund are among the 41 carriers listed on our insurance and financing page. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

Does a cleaning hurt?

We will not describe it as painless. Sensitive gums can be tender while they are cleaned, so tell the dentist or hygienist while it is happening. The pressure and the pace can be adjusted.
