If you have decided the moment passed, it did not. Teeth move at any age, because the biology that moves them does not retire.

What changes with age is the context around them. Gum health, old fillings, crowns and missing teeth all shape what is possible. That is a reason to be assessed properly, not a reason to stop asking.

Cost is part of the decision to start, and cost follows coverage. The ADA Health Policy Institute reports that 55% of adults aged 65 and older have no dental benefits, fetched 30 July 2026. Dental visits track coverage directly. In 2023, 53% of privately insured adults had a dental visit, against 16% of those with no dental insurance.

Waiting has its own price. Crowded teeth can be harder to clean, and a bite that is off can keep wearing in the same places.

What do adult braces cost?

We quote after an assessment. Adult cases vary too much for a published figure to mean anything. We do not publish treatment prices, and we will not give you a number that changes after we look.

Does insurance cover it?

Coverage for adult orthodontics depends on your plan. Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife and the Culinary Health Fund are among the carriers listed on our insurance and financing page. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

Does it hurt?

We will not describe orthodontic treatment as painless. Your clinician will tell you what to expect at the fitting and after each adjustment.

Can I have clear braces instead?

Which suits you depends on your case, and we will say so plainly at the assessment.
