Dental FAQs

Straight answers on cost, insurance, first visits, emergencies and treatment, before you pick up the phone.

What does a first visit cost?

New patients pay $45 for an exam and X-ray, or $29 if you are a senior.

Both prices were published on our own site on 30 July 2026.

What does treatment cost?

We use quote-only pricing. No treatment price is published on this site, because a real number needs an examination first. You get the figure after the examination.

Which insurance do you take?

Our insurance and financing page lists 41 named plans. They include Culinary Health Fund, Teachers Health Trust, Teamsters, UFCW, Nevada Medicaid and Health Plan of Nevada. Network status varies by office and by plan. Contact the office you want with your plan details before you book.

What if I have no insurance?

Whether the entry offer above carries any insurance condition is not published, so ask when you book. Treatment is quoted the same way for everyone, after an examination. Financing is available through Care Credit, Cherry, Sunbit and Lending Club, subject to the lender's approval.

What actually happens at a first visit?

An exam and X-rays, then a conversation about what was found. You leave knowing what is there and what the options are.

What should I bring?

Your insurance card, photo ID, and any recent X-rays from another practice. We take digital X-rays here, but recent films may still be usable.

Do you see emergencies?

Yes. Same-day and emergency appointments are available, and every one of our seven offices lists them.

How do I get seen fastest?

Call the office nearest you rather than booking online. Every office phone number is on the locations page and on that office's own page.

Which offices are open at the weekend?

That varies by office, so the hours on each location page are the answer. We do not state one site-wide schedule, because it would not be true for every office.

When should I call for a same-day appointment?

Our own published guidance names severe or persistent tooth pain, facial swelling, a pimple-like bump near a tooth, fever, or a bad taste. It also names a broken, chipped or knocked-out tooth, a lost filling, a loose crown, a broken denture, and a poking orthodontic wire. If swelling affects your breathing or swallowing, seek emergency medical care rather than waiting for a dental appointment.

How often do I need an exam and a cleaning?

That depends on your history, and we tell you after the first exam. Read dental exams and cleanings.

Do I need a filling or a crown?

Our own pages say a filling repairs a tooth affected by decay, cracks or fractures. A crown supports a broken, fractured or decaying tooth, a large or fractured filling, or a tooth after a root canal. We tell you which after the exam. Read composite fillings and crowns and caps.

My gums bleed when I brush. Is that a problem?

Our own FAQ lists bleeding gums among the signs of gum disease. It also says gum disease can be there without noticeable symptoms, so it is worth checking. Read periodontal disease and scaling and root planing.

Can I straighten my teeth without metal braces?

Invisalign uses clear aligners instead of brackets, though braces are still the right answer for some bites. Read Invisalign and braces for adults.

Will whitening work on my teeth?

That depends on what is causing the colour, and an examination tells us which kind you have. Read teeth whitening and porcelain veneers.

Do my wisdom teeth have to come out?

Not always. A digital X-ray shows the angle and the room available, and that is what decides it. Read wisdom teeth extractions.

Do you do everything in house?

General, cosmetic, restorative, surgical, periodontic and orthodontic care are all delivered in house, by our own clinicians. We use the SIROlaser for gum treatment and digital X-rays for imaging.

One practice, one clinical roster, seven offices across Las Vegas, North Las Vegas and Henderson. You are seen by that same roster at whichever office you visit.

Are the offices separately owned?

No. We own and run all seven ourselves. Where a group's offices are separately owned or separately branded, a consistent standard between them cannot be promised.

We are one practice that runs its own seven offices. We are not a network, not a referral service and not a franchise.
